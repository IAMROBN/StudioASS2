    About the Sounds

    Every factory patch here is generated by synthesis, not sampling. OmniWeb uses its own open JSON patch format (.owpatch) that you can read, edit, and share.
    OmniWeb cannot load Spectrasonics .prt_omn patches. Those files only reference Omnisphere's encrypted STEAM sample core and its proprietary engine — the sound lives in that protected library, not in the patch file. Use the sample loader above to bring in audio you own.
